If I could keep one thing...

If I could keep one thing from everything we've shared, it wouldn't be one particular day.

It would be the feeling that comes from knowing that somehow, out of all the people in this enormous world, our paths crossed.

I would keep the laughs. The random conversations. The little moments. The memories that didn't seem important when they happened but became important later.

And if this story has taught me anything, it's that the best parts aren't always the big moments. Sometimes they're the tiny ones. The ones we almost forget. The ones that quietly become our favorites.

So thank you. For being part of my story. For all the memories we've already made. And for all the ones we haven't made yet.

This isn't the end of our story. It's just the end of this chapter.
